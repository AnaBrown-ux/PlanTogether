Hiking @ Rattlesnake Ledge

Rattlesnake Ledge Trailhead, North Bend, WA

Sunday, 10 AM • Free

Posted by Salsabila A.

Description

Rattlesnake Ledge is a popular hiking trail with a steady climb through forested switchbacks leading to a scenic overlook. At the top, hikers are rewarded with sweeping views of Rattlesnake Lake and the surrounding mountains. It’s a relatively short but rewarding hike that’s great for beginners and groups.
